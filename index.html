About me

An engineer motivated by difficult problems, ownership and understanding what happens below the abstraction layer.

I am a C/C++ and Linux systems software engineer with a strong computer-science foundation. Curiosity has taken me from algorithms, object-oriented design and web applications to kernel programming, concurrency, networking and distributed storage.

I learn best by building: implementing a concept, testing it, understanding its trade-offs and connecting it to the larger system. Alongside systems work, I deliberately explore optimization, AI/NLP and backend development.
